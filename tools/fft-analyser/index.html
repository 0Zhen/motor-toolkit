About this tool

What it does
Upload an oscilloscope CSV or paste time-domain data to compute the frequency spectrum, extract harmonic amplitudes (1x–10x), and calculate THD — all in your browser, no install needed.
Window functions
Flat-top — best amplitude accuracy, ideal for THD measurement
Hann — general-purpose spectrum analysis
Hamming — low sidelobes, speech & telecom
Rectangular — best frequency resolution
Use cases
Motor back-EMF harmonic analysis · Power quality THD measurement · Audio distortion testing · Vibration analysis · FFT education
