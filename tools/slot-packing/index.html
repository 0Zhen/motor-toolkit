2 or more: the winding window splits into a left and a right coil side, each independently packed (double-layer winding). 1: the whole window is packed as one — doesn't decide anything about the total wire count, which is still turns × coils × strands.
Needle channel width [mm]
Gap reserved between the two coil sides (Coils/slot ≥ 2 only) — separate from liner thickness, since this is the phase-separator / needle-insertion clearance in the middle of the slot, not insulation against the slot wall. Shown below as a dashed rectangle.
Needle clearance [mm]
Safety margin between the wires and each side edge of the needle channel (Coils/slot ≥ 2 only): the gap between the two coil sides becomes needle width + 2 × clearance. Shown as dashed lines when > 0.

💡 Each wire is simulated settling into place, coming to rest against the slot wall (or the floor, for single-layer slots) and whichever wires are already placed — so it naturally hugs any wall angle and nests into the gaps between neighbors, without a fixed lattice pattern. For double-layer slots, each coil side settles independently toward its own outer wall. Still a geometric idealization, not a physics simulation — no wire tension, insertion order, friction, or enamel deformation.
